Wingstop Menu Prices and Combos

Updated 25 November 2026 · By WingstopMenus.us · Contact: contact@wingstopmenus.us

Wingstop prices are simple to understand once you know the structure: wings by the piece, combos that add fries and a drink, and group packs for larger orders. The numbers change by location, because Wingstop restaurants are franchised and set their own prices. This page explains the structure and gives the prices our site lists as a guide. To compare every item, Explore Wingstop menu prices on the full menu page.

What a combo includes

A Wingstop combo is wings plus seasoned fries, a dip, and a drink. You pick the wing count, the style, and the flavor, and the rest comes with it. The usual combo sizes are 6, 8, 10, and 15 pieces. A 6-piece combo is a light lunch. A 10-piece combo is a full meal for one hungry person. A 15-piece combo is a meal for two people who do not eat much, or one very hungry person.

You can build a combo with classic bone-in wings, boneless wings, or a mix of both. Tenders have their own combos too, with the same fries, dip, and drink. The sandwich has a combo version as well, with fries and a drink.

Prices the site lists

Our site lists a 10-piece classic order at $14.99 and a 10-piece boneless order at $13.49. Veggie sticks are listed at $1.79. These are the prices shown on the site as a guide, and they are location-dependent. Your local restaurant may charge more or less, and delivery apps usually charge more than the counter price.

That is why this page gives structure first and numbers second. If you know that a combo adds fries, a dip, and a drink to the wings, you can judge the value at any price. A combo is worth it when the fries, dip, and drink together cost less than buying them separately at the same store.

Which combo size is the best value

The best value is usually the size that matches your appetite, not the biggest one. A 15-piece combo is cheaper per wing than a 6-piece combo, but only if you eat all 15 pieces. Leftover wings are fine the next day, so a bigger combo is a good deal if you are happy to eat the rest later.

For two people, compare one 15-piece combo against two 8-piece combos. The single larger combo is often cheaper, and you still get two flavors if you split the wings. For one person, a 10-piece combo is the usual sweet spot.

Group packs and per-wing cost

Group packs of 30, 50, 75, and 100 pieces bring the per-wing cost down further, because you are buying in bulk and the sides are shared. A 30-piece pack is the smallest group option, and it is the one most often covered by deals. The Thursday and Sunday 30-wings-for-$30 offer, which runs through 3 January 2027, works out to about $1 per wing, which is one of the lowest per-wing prices Wingstop offers.

To work out any per-wing price, divide the total by the number of wings. A $14.99 order of 10 classic wings is about $1.50 per wing, and a $13.49 order of 10 boneless wings is about $1.35 per wing, using the site's listed prices. That quick sum tells you whether a deal is actually a deal.

How to check the price before you order

The most reliable price is the one in the Wingstop app or on the ordering page for your own store, because it shows the total for your location. The menu on this site is a guide to what to expect. If you are ordering for a group, check the price of the group pack and the price of the same wings as separate combos, and pick the cheaper one.

Questions people ask

How much is a 10-piece Wingstop order?

What comes in a Wingstop combo?
