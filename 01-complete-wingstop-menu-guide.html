Complete Wingstop Menu Guide

Updated 25 November 2026 · By WingstopMenus.us · Contact: contact@wingstopmenus.us

Wingstop is a United States chicken wing chain with 3,331 restaurants system-wide, and its menu is wider than most people expect. This guide walks through every part of it: wings by the piece, combos, tenders, sandwiches, sides, dips, drinks, and dessert. If you want prices and calories in one place, the Wingstop menu guide on our main site lists them side by side.

Wings by the piece

Wings are the core of the menu, and they come in two styles. Classic wings are bone-in wings, fried fresh and tossed in the flavor of your choice. Boneless wings are breaded pieces of chicken breast, also tossed in your flavor. Both are sold by the piece, so you can order 5, 10, 15, or more, depending on how hungry you are.

A plain classic wing has about 90 calories and 10 grams of protein. Sauce or dry rub adds a little more, so a classic wing lands at about 90 to 120 calories depending on the flavor. A boneless wing runs about 80 to 110 calories. The site lists a 10-piece classic order at $14.99 and a 10-piece boneless order at $13.49, but prices vary by location, and franchise restaurants set their own totals.

If you cannot decide between the two styles, order a mix. Many regulars split an order so everyone at the table gets the style they like, and you can pick a different flavor for each part of the order.

Combos and group packs

A combo bundles wings with seasoned fries, a dip, and a drink. The usual sizes are 6, 8, 10, and 15 pieces, and you can pick classic, boneless, or a mix of the two. Combos are the simplest way to order a full meal without thinking about each item separately.

For a group, the menu has larger packs of 30, 50, 75, and 100 pieces, with sides and dips included. The biggest packs let you pick several flavors in one order, which works well when people at the table want different heat levels. To see every combo and pack in one place, View the complete menu with current prices.

One practical tip: check the size of the pack against the number of people eating. Six to eight wings per adult is a reasonable plan for a main meal, and less if there are plenty of fries and sides on the table.

Tenders and the chicken sandwich

Crispy tenders are the boneless option for people who do not want to deal with bones at all. They are breaded chicken tenders, and you can order them plain or tossed in any of the 12 core flavors. Tenders come on their own or in a combo with fries, a dip, and a drink.

The chicken sandwich uses a fried chicken fillet on a bun, and it can also be tossed in a flavor. It is the smallest full meal on the menu, which makes it a good pick for a quick lunch. The sandwich is one of the cheapest single items on the menu, so it shows up often in budget orders.

Sides, dips, drinks, and dessert

Every Wingstop order can add seasoned fries, cheese fries, Louisiana Voodoo fries, buffalo ranch fries, Cajun fried corn, or veggie sticks. Veggie sticks are the lightest side on the menu, and the site lists them at $1.79. Dips include ranch, blue cheese, honey mustard, and cheese sauce, and they are worth ordering because the wings are fried fresh and the dip cools the heat.

Drinks run from fountain drinks and bottled water to Simply Lemonade. Dessert is a triple chocolate chunk brownie. None of these items is the reason to visit, but they round out a group order, and the brownie is the usual way to end a family meal.

How to order

You can order in the restaurant, through the Wingstop app or website, or through delivery services such as DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub. Delivery prices and fees are usually higher than in-store prices, so the total on an app is not the same as the total at the counter.

Before you order, check the menu for your own location. Prices, availability, and even some flavors vary by restaurant, because Wingstop locations are franchised. The menu on this site is a guide to what to expect, not a promise of the price at your store.

Questions people ask

How many flavors does Wingstop have?

Wingstop offers 12 core flavors: Atomic, Cajun, Garlic Parmesan, Hawaiian, Hickory Smoked BBQ, Hot Honey Rub, Lemon Pepper, Louisiana Rub, Mango Habanero, Mild, Original Hot, and Spicy Korean Q. Lemon Pepper is the number one seller.

How much is a 10-piece order?

The site lists a 10-piece classic order at $14.99 and a 10-piece boneless order at $13.49. Prices vary by location, so check your local restaurant for the exact total.

Does Wingstop deliver?

Yes. Wingstop delivers through its own app and website, and through DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub. Delivery fees and minimums vary by service and location.
