Wingstop Locations and Hours Guide

Updated 25 November 2026 · By WingstopMenus.us · Contact: contact@wingstopmenus.us

Wingstop has 3,331 restaurants system-wide as of the third quarter of 2026, with 2,787 in the United States and 544 international. That means most Americans live near one, but hours, prices, and even flavors vary by store, because Wingstop restaurants are franchised. This guide explains how to find a store, what the usual hours are, and what to check before you visit. For the full store list, see the Wingstop menu guide to locations on our main site.

How many Wingstop restaurants are there

As of the third quarter of 2026, Wingstop had 3,331 restaurants system-wide: 2,787 in the United States and 544 international, including United States territories. The chain is still growing, and new locations open every month, so the count changes often.

Most stores are in the southern and western United States, where the chain started, but there are now restaurants in most states. If you live in a smaller town, the nearest Wingstop may be a drive away, and it is worth checking the distance before you plan a visit.

New locations and opening hours

Wingstop opens new restaurants every month, and the chain reached 3,331 stores in the third quarter of 2026. A new store sometimes starts with shorter hours while the team settles in, and it may not offer delivery on its first day. If a store near you just opened, check its hours in the app rather than assuming the usual times.

Opening dates move, so treat any published date as an estimate until the store itself confirms it. If you are planning a visit to a brand-new store, call ahead. The phone number is listed on the store page, and a quick call avoids a wasted trip. New stores also tend to be busy in their first weeks, so ordering ahead in the app is a good idea.

Typical opening hours

Most Wingstop restaurants open between 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM and close between 11:00 PM and 1:00 AM. Hours vary by location, and some stores close earlier on Sundays or open later on holidays. The only reliable hours are the ones listed for your own store.

The busiest times are lunch, from about 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM, and dinner, from about 5:30 PM to 8:30 PM. Game days and weekends are busier still. If you want to skip the wait, order ahead in the app and pick a pickup time.

How to find a Wingstop near you

The fastest way is the official Wingstop store locator on wingstop.com, or the Wingstop app. Enter your city, state, or ZIP code, and it shows the nearest stores with hours, phone numbers, and whether they offer delivery, carryout, or dine-in.

Our locations page lists stores by state and city, with hours and contact details, and it is a useful starting point if you are comparing several stores. The official locator is still the best source for live information, because it shows openings, closures, and holiday hours.

Dine-in, carryout, and delivery

Most Wingstop restaurants offer dine-in, carryout, and delivery. Carryout is the cheapest option, because there is no delivery fee and the counter price is usually lower than the delivery app price. Dine-in is available at most stores, but Wingstop is mainly a takeout business, so seating is limited.

Delivery runs through the Wingstop app and website, and through DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub. Delivery fees and minimums vary by service and location, and delivery prices are usually higher than in-store prices.

What to check before you visit

Check four things before you go: the hours for your store, whether the flavor you want is available there, the price for your order at that location, and whether the store is open for dine-in or carryout only. A quick check in the app answers all four.

If anyone in your party eats gluten-free, check the guide first. Wingstop does not claim any product is gluten-free, and all fried foods are cooked in the same oil, so it is worth knowing before you order. You can read the full gluten-free guide on our main site.
