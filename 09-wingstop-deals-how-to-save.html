Wingstop Deals and How to Save

Updated 25 November 2026 · By WingstopMenus.us · Contact: contact@wingstopmenus.us

Wingstop runs deals most weeks, but they change often, and the best ones are easy to miss. This page explains the deals that are actually worth using, how Club Wingstop works, and the simple habits that cut the cost of every order. Prices and offers vary by location, so check the deals page before you order. The chicken sandwich is one of the cheapest single items, so View the complete menu if you want the lowest-cost meal on the menu.

The 30-for-$30 offer

The best Wingstop deal is the 30-wings-for-$30 offer, which runs on Thursdays and Sundays through 3 January 2027. At about $1 per wing, it is well below the usual per-wing price: a 10-piece classic order is listed at $14.99, which is about $1.50 per wing, and a 10-piece boneless order is listed at $13.49, or about $1.35 per wing.

The offer only runs on two days a week, so it rewards planning. If you can move a family dinner or a game night to a Thursday or a Sunday, the saving is real. Check the deals page to confirm the offer is still running at your store, because offers change and some stores do not take part.

Club Wingstop

Club Wingstop is the free loyalty program, and it is where the member-only offers live. Members have received free items with a purchase, early access to new flavors, and offers that are not available to everyone. Joining is free, and it takes a minute in the app.

The program is also how Wingstop releases limited items. The free chicken sandwich offer and the Wing Pass were both for Club Wingstop members first. If you order more than once a month, joining is worth it for the member offers alone.

Other deals to watch for

Wingstop runs bundle deals around big events, such as game days and holidays. These are usually group packs at a lower price, or a combo with a free side. They are worth checking when you are ordering for a group, because the saving on a big order is larger than on a single meal.

Delivery apps sometimes run their own Wingstop offers, such as a percentage off or free delivery. Compare the app price against the in-store price before you use one, because delivery prices are usually higher to begin with, and the discount may not cover the difference.

Deal habits that save money every week

The biggest saving comes from timing. The 30-for-$30 offer runs on Thursdays and Sundays, so plan family dinners for those days when you can. A reminder on Wednesday evening takes ten seconds and is worth a few dollars every week.

Join Club Wingstop before your next order, not after. Member offers only apply once you are signed up, and some of the best ones are for first-time members. Also compare the app total with the counter total before you pay: delivery prices are higher, so a deal that looks good on an app can be worse than the plain counter price.

  • Put a Thursday reminder on your calendar for the 30-for-$30 offer.
  • Join Club Wingstop before your next order.
  • Compare the app total with the counter total.
  • Order for pickup when the delivery fee is more than the saving.
  • Check whether the deal covers your store, because offers vary by location.

What to avoid

Avoid deals that make you buy more than you need. A discount on a 100-piece pack is not a saving if you only need 30 pieces. Avoid delivery when the fee is higher than the discount. And avoid assuming a deal is still running: offers end, and the price at the counter is the price that counts.

If you are ordering for a specific store, check the price for that store. Prices vary by location, and a deal that is good at one restaurant may not be available at another. To find your store and its hours, Learn more about Wingstop locations near you.

Questions people ask

What is the best Wingstop deal?

The 30-wings-for-$30 offer on Thursdays and Sundays is the best per-wing deal, at about $1 per wing. It runs through 3 January 2027.

Is Club Wingstop free?

Yes. Club Wingstop is free to join, and members get offers that are not available to everyone, including free items with a purchase.

Do Wingstop deals work on delivery apps?

Some do, but delivery prices are usually higher than in-store prices, so compare the total before you order. The saving from a deal can be smaller than the delivery fee.
